## What this shows

- Under the MA(29) null, with the first 20,000 draws of `dm_size_study.py` (seed 20261016): the selection 0.069, H1 0.095, H2 0.022, and H2 0.045 after Holm. With 20,000 replications and seed 20261016 these are the erratum's values.
- Mean autocorrelation over lags 30-120 (in brackets: the share of MA(29) series of the same length that reach it): the selection -0.118 (0.779), below the MA(29) mean; H1 0.126 (0.015), more than MA(29) produces at the 5 % level; H2 0.042 (0.080), above the MA(29) mean but within its range.
- At a nominal 5 % the pre-registered rule rejects at most 9.1 % of the MA(29) series; under the nulls built from the observed series it rejects from 1.7 % (the selection, Gaussian, Bartlett taper 120) to 17.0 % (H2, block bootstrap, 90 days).
- H1: 0.094-0.164 under the 8 nulls built from the observed series (0 below 0.05; MA(29) 0.095): not significant under any of them.
- H2: 0.018-0.077 under the 8 nulls built from the observed series (7 below 0.05). After Holm (MC SE at most 0.004): 0.045 under MA(29); 0.050 under the block bootstrap with blocks of 30 days and 0.036 under the Gaussian null with taper 29, the nulls with dependence up to about a month; and 0.068-0.155 under the 6 nulls with dependence over 45 days or more (0 below 0.05). Its significance after the size correction and Holm therefore holds only if the differential has no material dependence beyond about a month.
- The selection: 0.083-0.130 under the block bootstrap nulls and 0.014-0.068 under the Gaussian nulls. A correctly sized gate fails under MA(29) (0.069) and under 5 of the 8 nulls built from the observed series (0.068-0.130), and passes under the Gaussian nulls with tapers 60, 90 and 120 (0.014-0.049). Under these the rule rejects only 1.7-5.4 % of the null series: they carry the development differential's negative autocorrelation beyond lag 29 (section 1) into the null.
- When MA(29) is true the block bootstrap rejects 3.9-6.8 % of the series with 486 origins and 6.3-8.5 % of the series with 336 origins at a nominal 5 %. For MA(29) series with the committed statistic its median p-value is 0.060-0.073 for the selection (exact 0.069), 0.078-0.098 for H1 (exact 0.095), 0.020-0.027 for H2 (exact 0.022), at most 0.005 above the exact value: when MA(29) holds the method does not raise p-values materially. A single series' bootstrap p-value varies, though: the share of MA(29) series whose bootstrap p-value reaches the observed one is 0.001-0.281 for the selection, 0.100-0.312 for H1, 0.061-0.495 for H2.
- Under the block bootstrap with blocks of 30, 45 and 60 days the selection's bootstrap p-values (0.115-0.130) are larger than the bootstrap gives MA(29) series with the same statistic, although its dependence beyond lag 29 is below the MA(29) mean (section 1). The block bootstrap also keeps the marginal distribution, which is skewed for the selection (-2.28) and H2 (-2.09), while the MA(29) and Gaussian nulls are symmetric; this study does not separate that from the other explanations (the shape of the dependence within a month, level shifts).
